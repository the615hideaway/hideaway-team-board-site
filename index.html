How a job moves

  1. Sammy writes the job on the Sheet and, if it can wait, puts a TEAM event on the Calendar.
  2. The named teammate does the job.
  3. They update the Sheet status.
  4. They append the result to the Doc.

Status words

  • open — nobody has started it.
  • doing — someone is on it.
  • done — finished.
  • needs Sammy — waiting for a decision.
