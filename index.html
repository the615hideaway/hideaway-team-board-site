How a job moves

  1. Sammy tells one teammate, in that app, what he wants.
  2. That teammate writes the handoff in the Doc and adds a Sheet row for the other one.
  3. The other teammate reads it, does the work, and writes the result back in the Doc.
  4. When they need Sammy, the status is needs Sammy.

Status words

  • open — nobody has started it.
  • doing — someone is on it.
  • done — finished.
  • needs Sammy — waiting for a decision.
